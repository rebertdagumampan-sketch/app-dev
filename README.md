## My Favorite Anime: *I Want to Eat Your Pancreas*

**Original title:** Kimi no Suizō o Tabetai
**Genre:** Drama / Romance / Slice of life
**Type:** Anime film (2018), based on the novel by Yoru Sumino
**Studio:** Studio VOLN

### Why I Like It
Despite the odd title, it is a touching story about **friendship**, **time**, and *making the most of every day*. The title is not about eating anyone. It is a phrase the characters use to say they want to stay connected to someone.

### Main Characters
1. Sakura Yamauchi: a cheerful classmate with a pancreatic illness
2. The Classmate (Haruki Shiga): a quiet boy who finds her secret diary
3. Kyōko Takeda: Sakura's best friend

### Things I Love About It
- The emotional story
- The beautiful animation and music
- The message about living in the present
- The unexpected ending

> A story that reminds us that life is precious.
